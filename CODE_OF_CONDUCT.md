# Community conduct

BVMAC Market is a technical project built around public market information. Contributions are welcome from developers, analysts, designers, students and curious readers.

Keep discussions respectful, specific and useful. Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain assumptions, provide reproducible examples when reporting bugs, and avoid presenting model outputs as guaranteed financial outcomes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaks these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For a sensitive security issue, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
